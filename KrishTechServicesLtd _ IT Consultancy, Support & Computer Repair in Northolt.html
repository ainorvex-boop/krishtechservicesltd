How we work

A simple process, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Start the conversation

    Tell us what’s wrong or what you want to achieve. We’ll talk it through and say honestly whether we can help.

  2. Assess

    We look at the device, system or setup and explain what we find.

  3. Agree the plan

    You get a clear explanation of the work and the cost before anything starts.

  4. Fix and follow up

    We do the work, check it properly and stay available if anything isn’t right.
